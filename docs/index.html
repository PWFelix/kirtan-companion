Three ways in

Kirtan doesn't wait for a drummer.

Whether you lead the chanting, sing along, or are learning the drum yourself, Mrdanga Hero gives you a steady, living rhythm to lean on.

Play

Pick a beat and press play. Real recorded mridanga, dayan and bayan, with the gentle push and pull of human hands. Speed up with the chanting, tap the tempo, tune each side, and mix in kartals.

Learn

Strokes fall towards you, lane by lane: Ge and Khe on the bayan, Te and Ta on the dayan. Slow it right down, then bring it back up. There's a mirrored layout for left-handed players.

Make your own

Compose beats stroke by stroke in any meter, string them into the progression of a whole kirtan, and share them with a link. Sign in to keep them on all your devices and publish to the community.
