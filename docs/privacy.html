Privacy Policy

Last updated 9 November 2026

The short version. You can use Mrdanga Hero without an account, and then nothing about you leaves your device. If you choose to sign in, we store your email address, your display name and the beats you make, so they follow you between devices. There are no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and we never sell your information.

This policy explains how World Sankirtan Party (“we”, “us”) handles information in the Mrdanga Hero app for Android, iPhone and the web (the “app”) and on this website. Mrdanga Hero is a mridanga accompaniment and learning tool for kirtan.

Using the app without an account

Everything the app needs to play is built in: the drum sounds, the beats and the lessons. Without an account, the beats you create, your progressions and your settings (tempo, volumes, tuning, feel, left-handed layout and so on) are stored only on your device. We cannot see them.

When it has an internet connection, the app also downloads the current set of built-in beats from our server. That request carries no account or personal information.

If you sign in

Signing in is optional. It lets you keep your beats on all your devices and share them with the community. You can sign in with Google, or with an email address and password. When you do, we store:

WhatWhy
Your email addressTo identify your account and let you sign in.
Basic Google profile information, if you sign in with Google: your name, email address and profile picture link, as Google provides themTo create your account. Google passes this to our sign-in service when you sign in. We ask Google for nothing else: not your contacts, files, calendar or anything beyond your basic profile.
Your display nameShown as the author of anything you publish.
Your custom beats and progressions, and which progression is activeSo they sync between your devices.
Beats and progressions you choose to publish, and your ratings of others' published itemsTo run the community library.

What is public. Only what you choose to share. A published beat or progression, and your display name as its author, can be seen by anyone using the app, and anyone can save a copy to their own library. Ratings are shown only as an average and a count, not as who rated what. Your email address is never shown to other users.

Google user data

Mrdanga Hero's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We use your Google profile only to sign you in and to show your display name. We do not use it for advertising, do not sell it, and do not share it with anyone except the service providers below who run the app for us.

Sharing links

When you share a beat as a link, the beat itself is written into the link. It travels through whichever app you choose to send it with (WhatsApp, email and so on). We don't receive a copy unless the person you send it to opens it in the web version of the app, which reads it from the link to show it to them.

Device permissions

On Android the app uses the internet (to sync and fetch beats), a foreground media service and notifications (so the drum keeps playing, with playback controls, when your screen is locked), and a wake lock (so playback isn't interrupted). It does not use your microphone, camera, location, contacts or files.

What we don't do

  • No advertising, and no advertising identifiers.
  • No analytics, crash-reporting or tracking software in the app or on this website.
  • No cookies on this website.
  • We never sell or rent your information, and never use it to profile you.

Who processes your information

We use a small number of service providers to run the app. They handle data only on our behalf:

  • Supabase: hosts our database and sign-in service, which store the account information above.
  • Google: provides “Sign in with Google”, if you choose it.
  • GitHub: hosts this website. Like any web host, it may record your IP address in server logs for security and reliability.

Our hosting providers record standard technical information, such as IP addresses and request times, in short-lived logs to keep their services secure and working. Your information may be stored and processed outside your country, by providers that protect it under their own security and privacy commitments.

Keeping and deleting your information

We keep your account information for as long as you have an account. You can delete individual beats and progressions, and unpublish anything you've published, in the app at any time.

To delete your account, open an issue titled “Delete my account” on our GitHub issues page. Please don't post your email address or other personal details there. We'll reply and arrange to confirm privately that the account is yours. When an account is deleted, everything linked to it is deleted too: your beats, progressions, published items and ratings. Published items are taken out of the community library.

What deleting your account can't undo. When you share a beat or progression, you license it to other users (see our Terms). Copies other people have already saved to their own libraries belong to their accounts, and links you have sent still contain the beat. These are not removed when you delete your account. Your email address is never part of a shared item.

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, export or delete your information, or to object to how it's used. You can ask us to exercise any of them the same way.

Children

Mrdanga Hero is for people of all ages to chant and learn, and you don't need an account to use it. The optional account is not intended for children under 13, or the minimum age in your country, without a parent or guardian's involvement. If you believe a child has given us personal information without that, contact us and we'll delete it.

Security

Information travels to our servers encrypted (HTTPS). Database access rules let each account read and change only its own private data. Sign-in with Google happens in your browser, so the app never sees your Google password.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top. If a change is significant, we'll also tell you in the app.

Contact

World Sankirtan Party. Questions about privacy: please open an issue on our GitHub issues page.
